Delete it in the game (immediate)

  1. Open Dungeon Core Summoner.
  2. Tap the gear icon at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted at once. This cannot be undone.

Or ask us by email

If you can no longer open the game, email dcs.game.support@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”. Include your Player ID (shown in Settings, it looks like DCS-XXXX-XXXX) or the email address linked to the account. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account identifier, linked email address and sign-in links.
  • Your display name, save data, collection, progress, rankings and guild membership.
  • Gameplay events, error reports, problem reports and survey answers linked to the account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records — kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically 7 years. They are not used for anything else.
  • Backups — deleted data is purged from backups within 30 days.
  • Enforcement records — if an account was banned, the minimum data needed to enforce the ban.

Purchases already made are not refunded when you delete your account. See the Purchase Terms and the Privacy Policy.
